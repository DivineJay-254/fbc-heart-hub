Free Block Creatives is a refugee-led arts, livelihoods and community development collective. We create safe, inclusive spaces where youth, LGBTQ+ individuals and gender minorities learn, create and earn with dignity.

Our vision

A creative, inclusive and empowered community where refugees use arts, culture and skills to express themselves, sustain livelihoods and live with dignity.

Our mission

We provide safe, inclusive and transformative spaces for refugees to learn, create and produce artistic and livelihood-based work through arts education, creative production and skills training.

What we make possible

Creative practice with a practical purpose.

Our work connects expression with opportunity, making room for people to build confidence, community and income on their own terms.

01

Safe spaces

Welcoming studios and workshops where young people and marginalized artists can meet, experiment and be heard.

02

Real skills

Hands-on learning in performance, film, design, beadwork and craft production led by community practitioners.

03

Shared income

Creative products, commissions and showcases that help artists turn talent into dignified livelihood pathways.

From Kakuma, outward

Local talent deserves a wider stage.

Free Block Creatives is building a platform for refugee artists to shape their own narratives. We bring together creative education, production and enterprise so that a workshop can become a performance, a product or a new possibility.

Every project begins with listening to the community and grows through collaboration with artists, partners and supporters.
