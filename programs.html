Programs & Activities

From learning, to production, to income.

Three connected departments help refugee artists develop skills, tell stories and create sustainable livelihoods.

Program A

Arts Education & Training

  • Teen and student holiday programs
  • Film, choreography, writing and performance training
  • Creative workshops and mentorship
  • Garden practice and farming

Program B

Production & Creative Works

  • Film and media production
  • Music composition and choreography
  • Scriptwriting, performances and showcases
  • Community exhibitions and presentations

Program C

Livelihoods & Skills

  • Beadwork and textile design
  • Crochet and related crafts
  • Product development and quality control
  • Income-generating creative production
